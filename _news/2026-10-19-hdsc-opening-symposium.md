Symposium update: I will present our joint project, [_Learning and Dynamics in Stochastic Multi-Agent Systems_](https://chn.tum.de/research/heilbronn-data-science-center), at the Scientific Opening Symposium of the Heilbronn Data Science Center in Heilbronn on November 2, 2026. Led by Jingui Xie, Martin Bichler, Christian Kuehn, and Christoph Knochenhauer, the project combines data science, AI, operations research, management, and economics to study learning and adaptation in stochastic multi-agent systems, with applications in healthcare, service systems, and platform economies.
